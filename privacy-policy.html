Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 12 November 2026

Textora is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site, how it is used, and what rights you have. It is written to be accurate for the current version of the site as described below.

The short version: all text processing on Textora runs in your browser. The text you type or paste is not uploaded to any server controlled by this site. It stays in your browser tab unless you copy or download it. See sections 3 and 4 for the details.

1. Data Controller and Contact

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and other applicable data protection laws, the data controller responsible for this website is:

Textora — independent publisher
Email: textora1counter@gmail.com

Textora is an independent website run by a single publisher. It is not part of a company or media organization. All questions about this policy, data processing, or your rights can be sent to the email address above. Messages are read by the site operator and answered where a reply is appropriate.

2. Scope of This Policy

"Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. An identifiable person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or one or more specific characteristics.

"Usage data" is data required to use the website. On Textora, usage data is limited to the technical request information described in section 3. The site does not build user profiles, does not track you across sites, and does not require an account.

3. Automatic Data Collection via Website Access

When you visit a page on Textora, your browser sends a request to the hosting provider. Textora is hosted on Vercel Inc. As part of serving the page, Vercel may record standard technical information, including:

  • Date and time of access.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system.
  • The URL of the page on Textora that was requested.
  • The referring URL (the page that linked you to Textora).
  • Approximate geographic location derived from IP address (country or region).
  • The requesting IP address.
  • Response status code and request duration.

This information is used by Vercel for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and basic operational logging. It is processed under Vercel's privacy practices, not by Textora directly. You can read Vercel's privacy documentation at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Textora does not associate any of this data with any individual and does not use it for profiling or advertising.

4. How Text Processing Works

All text-processing features on Textora — word counting, character counting, sentence and paragraph counting, reading and speaking time, keyword density, case conversion, whitespace cleanup, sorting, comparison, Lorem Ipsum generation — run entirely in your browser using JavaScript.

This means:

  • The text you enter is not uploaded to any server controlled by Textora.
  • There is no backend process that receives your text, stores it, or analyzes it.
  • When you close the tab or navigate away, the text is discarded by your browser.
  • No one — including the site operator — can retrieve text you have not explicitly saved or downloaded.

Because the site does not receive your text, it cannot be used to build a profile of you. This applies whether the text is a shopping list or a confidential document.

5. Downloads and Copies

Many tools provide a "Download .txt" button. When you use it, a plain-text file is created in your browser and saved to your device. Textora does not receive a copy. The file is under your control and subject to the storage and backup practices of your device.

The "Copy" button copies text to your device's clipboard. The clipboard is managed by your operating system; Textora does not read from it except when you explicitly trigger a "Paste" button, and even then the text never leaves your browser.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Textora itself does not set any first-party cookies and does not use browser local storage for tracking, profiling, or advertising purposes.

Vercel may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies or similar technical identifiers to support its infrastructure — for example, for load balancing, security, and abuse prevention. These cookies are not used to track you across sites and are typically short-lived. You can read Vercel's cookie documentation at the same link as above.

If future versions of Textora introduce analytics or advertising, this policy will be updated before that change is deployed, and the Cookie Policy will list the specific cookies used, their names, purposes, and retention periods.

7. Analytics

Textora does not currently use any analytics service. No Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, or similar service is loaded, and no analytics cookies or identifiers are set.

If analytics is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated before the change is deployed. The update will name the provider, describe what is measured (for example, aggregate page views, referrers, or Core Web Vitals), list the cookies or identifiers used, and explain how users can opt out where applicable.

8. Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled on Textora. No Google AdSense or other advertising script is loaded, and no advertising cookies are set.

If advertising is enabled in the future — for example, after approval from Google AdSense — this policy will be updated before any ad scripts are loaded. When that happens:

  • Advertising networks (for example, Google AdSense) may set cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.
  • These networks may collect data such as approximate location, browser type, pages visited, and other information, according to their own privacy policies.
  • Users in regions that require consent for advertising cookies will be shown a consent mechanism before any ad-related cookie is set.
  • You will be able to opt out of personalized advertising through the ad network's settings (for Google, at adssettings.google.com).

For more information about how Google uses data when you use sites that display its ads, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

9. Email Contact

If you email the site operator at textora1counter@gmail.com, your email address and the contents of your message are retained only as long as needed to respond and to keep an accurate record of corrections or accessibility reports.

Your email address is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. It is not added to any mailing list. There is no newsletter. If you ask for your correspondence to be deleted, the operator will delete it unless a legal obligation requires retention.

10. Data Retention

Because Textora does not store the text you enter into its tools, there is nothing for the site to delete. Text you download remains on your device until you delete it. Hosting logs held by Vercel are retained according to Vercel's policies. Email correspondence is retained only as described in section 9.

11. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding any personal data the site may process about you:

  • Right of access. You can request a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Right to rectification. You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure. You can request that your data be deleted from records.
  • Right to restriction of processing. You can request that processing of your data be restricted.
  • Right to object. You can object to certain types of processing, such as direct marketing.
  • Right to data portability. Where processing is based on consent or contract and is carried out by automated means, you can request the data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to lodge a complaint. You can file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because Textora does not store text you enter into the tools, most requests will resolve to "no record exists." Email correspondence is the main category of data the site actually holds, and it can be deleted on request.

To exercise any of these rights, write to textora1counter@gmail.com. Requests are handled within one month as required by GDPR.

12. Children's Privacy

Textora is a general-purpose utility site and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions). The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information by email, contact the site operator and it will be deleted.

13. Third-Party Services

The site currently uses one third-party service: Vercel Inc. for hosting, as described in section 3. When advertising is enabled in the future, Google AdSense or a similar advertising network will become an additional third-party service, as described in section 8. No other third-party services are loaded on the site at this time.

Links to external websites do not imply endorsement, and this policy does not cover the practices of those external sites. Read their privacy policies before providing personal information to them.

14. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the site's functionality, legal requirements, or the services it uses. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version. Significant changes — such as enabling analytics or advertising — will be announced on the homepage and the effective date will be updated.

The current version of the policy is always available at this URL.

15. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the text I enter stored on your servers?

No. All text processing happens in your browser. The text you type or paste into a tool is not uploaded to any server controlled by Textora. When you close the tab or navigate away, the text is discarded by your browser.

Can I recover text I lost after closing the tab?

No. Because the site never receives your text, it has no way to retrieve it. If you want to keep a piece of text, use the "Download .txt" button in the tool before closing the tab. Textora cannot recover text that was never saved to your device.

Do you claim ownership of the text I enter?

No. You retain full ownership of anything you enter. Textora does not claim ownership, license rights, or any other interest in your content. The site also does not have access to it.

Can I use the outputs (for example, generated Lorem Ipsum) commercially?

Yes. Lorem Ipsum output, sorted lists, converted text, and other tool outputs are yours to use freely, provided your use does not infringe any third-party rights or applicable law. Remember that Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text with no meaning and should never be published as real content.

Do you use cookies?

Textora itself does not set first-party cookies. The hosting provider, Vercel, may set strictly necessary technical cookies for infrastructure purposes. See the Cookie Policy for detail.

Do you use Google Analytics?

No. No analytics service is currently installed on the site. If analytics is added in the future, this policy will be updated before the change is deployed.

Are there ads on Textora?

Not currently. Advertising is disabled. If ads are enabled after AdSense approval, this policy will be updated, and users in regions that require consent will see a consent mechanism before any ad-related cookie is set.

How do I contact you about privacy?

Email textora1counter@gmail.com. Messages about privacy, data deletion, or corrections are prioritized.
