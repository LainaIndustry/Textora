Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Textora (the "site"), which is hosted on Vercel and operated by the site owner. It describes what is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Text Processing Happens in Your Browser

All counting, formatting, comparison and analysis features on this site run locally in your browser with JavaScript. The text you type or paste into a tool is not sent to a server by this site. It remains in your browser tab unless you copy or download it, close the tab, or refresh the page.

The site does not store your text, does not log your text, and has no way to retrieve text you have not saved yourself.

2. Hosting on Vercel

Textora is hosted on Vercel Inc. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process standard technical information when you load a page, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • The requested URL and referring URL
  • Date and time of the request
  • Response status and response time

This information is used by Vercel for security, reliability, abuse prevention and basic operational purposes. It is processed under Vercel's privacy practices, not by Textora directly. You can read Vercel's privacy documentation at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Vercel may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies or similar technical identifiers to support its infrastructure (for example, for load balancing or security). These are operational, not tracking or advertising cookies.

3. No Accounts, No Backend, No Uploaded Text

Textora does not require registration or sign-in. There is no user database, no content stored on our side, and no server-side storage of the text you enter. The site does not use paid APIs to process your content.

4. Analytics

Textora does not currently use any analytics service. No Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Plausible, Fathom or similar service is loaded on the site, and no analytics cookies are set.

If analytics is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated before the change is deployed. The update will name the provider, describe what is measured, list the cookies or similar technologies used, and explain how users can opt out where applicable.

5. Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled on Textora. No Google AdSense or other advertising script is loaded, and no advertising cookies are set.

If advertising is enabled after AdSense approval, this policy will be updated before any ad scripts are loaded. When that happens:

  • Advertising networks (for example, Google AdSense) may set cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.
  • Those networks may collect data such as approximate location, browser type and pages visited, according to their own privacy policies.
  • Users in regions that require consent for advertising cookies will be shown a consent mechanism before any ad-related cookie is set.

For more information about Google's data practices in relation to advertising, see policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

In its current configuration, Textora itself does not set any cookies and does not use browser local storage for tracking purposes. Vercel may set strict, technical cookies as described in Section 2. See the Cookie Policy for detail.

7. Data Retention

Textora does not store the text you enter into its tools. If you use the download feature, the resulting file is saved on your own device, not on the site. Hosting logs held by Vercel are retained according to Vercel's policies.

8. Contact by Email

If you email the site operator at textora1counter@gmail.com, your email address and the contents of your message are retained only as long as needed to respond, and to keep an accurate record of corrections or accessibility reports. Your email address is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

9. Children

Textora does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can use Textora without providing any personal information. You can block or clear cookies through your browser settings. You can contact the site operator by email to ask what information, if any, is associated with your messages, or to request deletion of correspondence you have sent. Because the site does not maintain a user database, most data-subject requests will resolve to "no record exists."

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes, such as enabling analytics or advertising, will be announced on the homepage or in a changelog before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to textora1counter@gmail.com.
