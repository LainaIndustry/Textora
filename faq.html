Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the tools, privacy and how counts are calculated.

Word and Character Counting

How is a word counted?

Text is split on whitespace. Each sequence of characters separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks counts as one word. Punctuation attached to a word does not create a separate word. This is a mechanical rule and may not match every language's tokenization.

Are spaces included in character counts?

Both are shown. "Characters including spaces" counts every character. "Characters excluding spaces" removes whitespace before counting.

How are sentences counted?

Sentences end at a period, exclamation mark or question mark followed by whitespace or the end of the text. Non-empty text with no ending punctuation counts as one sentence.

How are paragraphs counted?

Paragraphs are separated by blank lines. A single block of text with no blank line is one paragraph.

Why do different tools show different counts?

Tools apply different rules for punctuation, hyphenated words, emoji and non-Latin scripts. Small differences are normal.

Reading and Speaking Time

How is reading time estimated?

Word count divided by an assumed words-per-minute rate. The default is 238 WPM. You can change it on the Reading Time Calculator.

How is speaking time estimated?

Word count divided by an assumed speaking rate. The default is 150 WPM. Adjust it on the Speaking Time Calculator.

Why is speaking slower than reading?

Speaking involves articulation, pauses and audience awareness. Most speakers deliver at 120–180 WPM.

Privacy and Data

Is my text uploaded to a server?

The tools process text locally in your browser. The site does not send your text to a server. See the Privacy Policy for details about hosting and future services.

Does the site work offline?

After a page loads, counting and text processing run in your browser without network requests. Reloading a page requires a connection unless the browser has cached it.

Do you use cookies?

Textora itself does not set cookies in its current configuration. The hosting provider (Vercel) may set strict, technical cookies for infrastructure. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Are there ads?

Advertising is currently disabled. If enabled after AdSense approval, ads will be clearly separated from content. See the Privacy Policy.

Who operates the site?

Textora is an independent website operated by a single publisher. See the About page and the Editorial Policy for details.

Tools and Features

Can I download my text?

Yes. The main editor and most tools have a "Download .txt" button.

Does the tool support long documents?

Browser text areas can handle long documents. Very large texts may slow down live statistics in older devices.

Can I use the tools on mobile?

Yes. The site is responsive and the editor works on mobile browsers.

Does the keyword density tool count phrases?

No. It analyzes individual words. Phrase-level analysis requires a different approach.

Why does the text comparison tool show line-level changes?

It compares lines rather than words. A word-level change appears as a removed line plus an added line.
