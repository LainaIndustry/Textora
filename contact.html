Contact Textora

Questions, corrections, accessibility feedback and general enquiries are welcome. The fastest way to reach the site operator is by email.

Email textora1counter@gmail.com
Open email app

What You Can Contact About

Corrections

Found a typo, a wrong count, or an article that is out of date? Include the page URL and the specific text.

Accessibility

If a page or tool is hard to use with a keyboard or screen reader, describe the barrier and your browser.

Questions about the tools

If a count or result looks wrong, include the text you pasted and what you expected to see.

General enquiries

Questions about the site, its policies or its content are welcome.

What to Include in Your Email

To help you get a useful reply, please include:

  • The page URL you are writing about, if your message relates to a specific page or tool.
  • A clear description of the issue, question or suggestion.
  • The text or steps to reproduce if the issue involves a tool result that looks wrong.
  • Device and browser if the issue is visual or accessibility-related.

About Contact Forms

Textora does not run a contact form. A form on a static website cannot send messages without a backend server or a paid form service, and offering one that fails silently would be misleading. Email is used instead because it is delivered to a real, monitored inbox and replies can be sent directly.

Response time: the site operator reads every message. Corrections and accessibility reports are prioritised. Replies are usually sent within a few business days, though a reply cannot be guaranteed for every message.

What Is Not Answered

The following are not answered and will be ignored:

  • Guest post and link exchange requests.
  • Paid placement or sponsored content proposals.
  • Requests for bulk data or scraped content.
  • Messages that are abusive, threatening or illegal.

Privacy

When you send an email, your address and the message are retained only as long as needed to respond and to keep an accurate record of corrections or accessibility reports. Your address is not sold, shared with advertisers, or added to any mailing list. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.
