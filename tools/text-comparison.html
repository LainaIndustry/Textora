Text Comparison Tool

Compare two versions and highlight added and removed lines.

Differences

How the Comparison Works

The tool compares the two texts line by line using a longest common subsequence approach. Lines that appear only in the revised text are marked as additions; lines that appear only in the original are marked as removals. Unchanged lines are shown without highlighting.

When to Use It

  • Reviewing edits between two drafts.
  • Checking whether a revision changed specific lines.
  • Comparing exported versions of the same content.

Limitations

Comparison is line-based. A word-level change within a line is shown as a removed line plus an added line. Very large documents may take longer to compare.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the comparison case-sensitive?

Yes. Lines must match exactly, including case and whitespace.

Can I compare word by word?

Not with this tool. It compares lines. Use Find and Replace or a diff tool for word-level detail.

Does it store my text?

No. Comparison happens locally in your browser.
