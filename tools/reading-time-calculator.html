How Reading Time Is Estimated

Reading time is calculated as word count divided by your chosen words-per-minute rate. It is a rough estimate: sentence complexity, subject familiarity, formatting and device all affect real reading speed.

Choosing a WPM Rate

  • Light content: around 250–300 WPM.
  • Standard articles: around 220–250 WPM.
  • Technical or dense material: around 150–200 WPM.
  • Careful reading or study: often below 150 WPM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my reading time different from another site?

Different tools use different default WPM rates and word-counting rules. Adjust the WPM field to match the assumption you want.

Does reading time include images?

No. Only the text in the editor is used. Images, videos and interactivity add time that this tool does not model.

Can I use this for a presentation?

Use the Speaking Time Calculator instead. Speaking pace is usually slower than reading pace.
