How Case Conversion Works

Uppercase and lowercase use JavaScript's built-in case conversion. Title case capitalizes the first letter of each whitespace-separated word and lowercases the rest. Sentence case lowercases everything and then capitalizes the first letter of the text and letters after ., ! or ?.

When to Use Each Style

  • Uppercase: acronyms, warnings, short labels.
  • Lowercase: casual text, usernames, certain brand styles.
  • Title case: headings, article titles, book titles.
  • Sentence case: body text, subtitles and UI labels.

Limitations

Title case does not understand small words like “of” or “the” in the way a style guide might. For professional publishing, apply your publication's style guide after conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does title case handle hyphenated words?

Each side of the hyphen is treated as part of the same word, so only the first letter is capitalized. Some style guides capitalize both parts.

Will it preserve line breaks?

Yes. Only letter casing changes; line breaks and spacing are preserved.

Does sentence case fix abbreviations?

No. It uses simple punctuation rules and may capitalize after abbreviations like “Dr.” or “e.g.” incorrectly.
