How Speaking Time Is Estimated

Speaking time is word count divided by your chosen words-per-minute rate. Pauses, transitions, audience interaction and nervousness are not modelled. Use this as a planning starting point and rehearse aloud with a timer.

Typical Speaking Rates

  • Slow, clear delivery: around 120–140 WPM.
  • Conversational presentation: around 150–170 WPM.
  • Fast or energetic delivery: around 180–200 WPM.
  • Auctioneer-style: far above normal speaking pace; not recommended for clarity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this estimate?

It depends on the speaker. Most people speak faster when rehearsing alone and slower in front of an audience. Rehearse aloud and adjust your rate.

Does it account for pauses?

No. Add a small buffer for pauses, transitions and audience reaction if you need a realistic stage time.
