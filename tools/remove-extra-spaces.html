Remove Extra Spaces

Collapse repeated spaces and stray whitespace in pasted text.

Cleaned result

What Gets Cleaned

  • Runs of spaces and tabs are collapsed to a single space.
  • Spaces before and after line breaks are removed.
  • Three or more consecutive line breaks are reduced to two.
  • Leading and trailing whitespace is removed.

When to Use It

Text copied from PDFs, web pages and email often contains double spaces or inconsistent gaps. Cleaning whitespace makes the text easier to reuse in a CMS or document editor. See How to Remove Extra Spaces from Text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it remove intentional indentation?

Yes. Spaces at the start of lines are treated as whitespace and removed. Preserve indentation manually if you need it.

Does it change line breaks?

It reduces runs of three or more line breaks to two, but keeps single and double breaks.
