How Frequency Is Counted

The text is lowercased, most punctuation is removed, and words are split on whitespace. Each unique word's count is recorded. The list shows the top 25 words by count. This is a word-level analysis, not phrase-level.

When to Use It

  • Spotting accidental word repetition in a draft.
  • Reviewing vocabulary variety.
  • Checking whether a term dominates a document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it treat “Hello” and “hello” as the same word?

Yes. Words are lowercased before counting.

Does it count phrases?

No, only individual words.

Why are only 25 rows shown?

The panel shows the top 25 most frequent words to keep the display readable. The underlying counts are complete.
