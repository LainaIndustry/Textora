Sentences are counted at ., ! and ?. A non-empty text with no ending punctuation counts as one sentence.

How Sentences Are Counted

The counter looks for sequences of ending punctuation followed by whitespace or the end of the text. Abbreviations and decimal numbers may create extra counts; this is a known limitation of mechanical sentence detection.

Why Sentence Count Matters

Very long sentences are harder to read. If your average sentence length is high, consider splitting sentences for clarity. Compare this count with the Word Counter to see your average words per sentence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does an abbreviation like “Dr.” count as a sentence?

It can. The counter treats a period followed by a space as a possible sentence end. For texts with many abbreviations, treat the result as approximate.

What about ellipsis (…)?

Three dots in a row are treated as one ending sequence. If they are followed by more text, the count may or may not include a new sentence depending on spacing.

How is average sentence length calculated?

Divide the word count by the sentence count. Our tool shows both numbers so you can do this manually.
