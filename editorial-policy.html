Editorial Policy

Last updated: [DATE TO BE SET BY SITE OWNER]

Purpose

This policy describes how the articles and tool instructions on this site are created and maintained. It applies to all written content on the site.

Principles

  • Usefulness first: content should help the reader accomplish a task or understand a concept.
  • Accuracy: claims about tools, calculations and functionality reflect what the site actually does.
  • Clarity: writing aims to be readable by its intended audience, without unnecessary jargon.
  • Honesty: no fabricated statistics, references, quotes, testimonials or authors.
  • Originality: content is written for this site, not copied from competitors.

Creating Articles

Each article is planned before it is written:

  1. Identify the reader's question or task.
  2. Outline the answer with clear sections.
  3. Draft with practical examples.
  4. Review for accuracy against the tools on the site.
  5. Edit for clarity and length.
  6. Add internal links to relevant tools and articles.

Tool Instructions

Tool pages describe what each tool does, how to use it and what its limitations are. When tool behavior changes, the instructions and limitations must be updated to match. Any claim about accuracy, privacy or calculation method is reviewed against the actual implementation.

Corrections

Errors are corrected when identified. Corrections may be made silently for small typos or with a note for substantive changes. If you find an error, please report it through the Contact page.

Updates

Articles may be updated to reflect changes in tools, best practices or reader feedback. The page does not display fabricated publication dates; any dates shown reflect actual work on the page.

Author Attribution

Content on this site is attributed to the Textora Editorial Team. Individual author names are not invented. If a named author is added later, their real identity and role must be documented.

Advertising and Editorial Independence

Advertising, when enabled, is separated from editorial content. Ad placements do not influence which topics are covered or what the content says. The site does not publish sponsored content without clear labeling.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@YOURDOMAIN.com.
