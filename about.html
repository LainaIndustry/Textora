About Textora

Purpose

Textora provides free, browser-based text tools for students, teachers, writers, bloggers, researchers, editors and anyone who works with written content. The site focuses on clear counts, useful text transformations and practical guidance. All primary tools run locally in the browser without a backend server.

Who This Site Is For

These tools are designed for people who need reliable numbers or quick text cleanup without installing software or creating an account. That includes:

  • Students checking assignment word limits.
  • Teachers preparing handouts and lesson material.
  • Writers and bloggers reviewing post length and readability.
  • SEO professionals inspecting term frequency as one descriptive signal.
  • Editors cleaning pasted text and comparing drafts.
  • Business and marketing professionals fitting copy into character limits.

What Tools Are Provided

The site includes a word counter, character counter, sentence and paragraph counters, reading and speaking time calculators, a keyword density checker, case converters, whitespace and line-break tools, a sorter, a text reverser, a find-and-replace tool, a text comparison tool, a word frequency counter and a Lorem Ipsum generator. Each tool has its own page with instructions, examples and related links.

How to Use the Tools

Open any tool from the homepage or the navigation menu. Paste or type your text into the input area. Statistics and results update as you type or after you press a button, depending on the tool. Most tools offer a copy button and many offer a download option. Nothing requires registration.

What This Site Aims to Improve

The goal is to make routine text checks faster: knowing the length of a draft, cleaning up copied text, spotting repetition and estimating reading or speaking duration. The site also aims to explain what these metrics mean and where they have limits, so users can make informed editing decisions.

What the Site Does Not Do

Textora does not provide professional editing, legal, academic or SEO advice. Automated counts and estimates are mechanical and can differ from other tools. The site does not guarantee search rankings, advertising approval or specific outcomes from using the tools.
