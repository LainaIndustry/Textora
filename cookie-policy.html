Cookie Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a site. They can remember preferences, track sessions or support analytics and advertising. Similar technologies include browser local storage and pixel tags.

2. Cookies Used by Textora

In its current configuration, Textora does not set any first-party cookies and does not use browser local storage for tracking purposes. The site does not load any advertising or analytics scripts that would set cookies.

3. Hosting Cookies (Vercel)

Textora is hosted on Vercel Inc. Vercel may set a small number of strictly necessary, operational cookies or similar technical identifiers to support infrastructure functions such as load balancing, security and abuse prevention. These are not used for tracking, profiling or advertising, and are typically short-lived.

For details about the cookies Vercel sets on hosted sites, see Vercel's privacy documentation.

4. Analytics

Textora does not currently use an analytics service. No Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Plausible, Fathom or similar service is loaded, and no analytics cookies are set.

If analytics is enabled in the future, this page will be updated before the change is deployed. The update will name the provider, list the cookie names and retention periods, describe what is measured, and explain how users can opt out where applicable.

5. Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled on Textora. No Google AdSense or other advertising script is loaded, and no advertising cookies are set.

If advertising is enabled after AdSense approval, this page will be updated before any ad scripts are loaded. When that happens, the following will be documented here:

  • The advertising provider (for example, Google AdSense).
  • The cookies or similar technologies it uses.
  • The consent mechanism shown to users in regions where consent is required for advertising cookies.
  • How users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising.

Google provides information about how it uses cookies in advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control or clear cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions specific to your browser and device.

Because Textora itself does not set cookies in its current configuration, clearing cookies on Textora mainly affects any operational cookies set by the hosting provider.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the site begins to use new cookies, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. Significant changes will be announced on the homepage.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to textora1counter@gmail.com.
